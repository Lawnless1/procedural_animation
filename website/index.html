THIS POND

This page is a living sketch: a koi pond rendered procedurally. Scroll, and day falls into night.

It's my take on constraint chains: every fish is dragged through the water by its head, schooling by boid rules over a simulated wave grid, all drawn fresh each frame. Press the button at the bottom right to see how every fish is put together.

Sparked by an interesting video I saw.
